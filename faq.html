Category 1

Admissions & Applications

How early should I start the university application process?
We strongly recommend starting the application process 10 to 12 months before your intended intake. This allows ample time for university shortlisting, standardized test preparation, SOP drafting, and resolving any missing documentation.
Do I absolutely need to take the IELTS or TOEFL exams?
In most cases, yes. If you are applying to an English-speaking country and your first language is not English, proficiency tests are mandatory. However, some universities may waive this requirement if your previous degree was taught entirely in English.
Can I apply to universities before taking standardized tests like the GRE/GMAT?
Yes, many universities offer conditional offers. You can submit your core application and transcripts first, and then provide your standardized test scores once they are available, provided it is before their final deadline.
How many universities should I realistically apply to?
We typically advise our students to apply to 6 to 8 universities. This list should be strategically divided into 2 ambitious "reach" schools, 3-4 "target" schools where you perfectly match the profile, and 1-2 "safe" schools.
Do you write the Statement of Purpose (SOP) for me?
No, we do not write SOPs from scratch, as universities use powerful AI to detect ghostwriting. Instead, we provide brainstorming sessions, detailed structural frameworks, and multiple rounds of rigorous editing to polish your original thoughts.
Does your consultancy guarantee university admission?
While we boast a 98% overall success rate, ethical guidelines prevent us from guaranteeing admission to any specific university. However, our Premium and Elite packages do include a money-back guarantee if you receive zero admits from your tailored list.
Category 2

Visas & Financial Planning

Are there scholarships available for international students?
Absolutely. Many institutions offer merit-based scholarships (based on GPA and test scores) and need-based financial aid. Additionally, there are government-funded scholarships like the Fulbright or Chevening programs. We guide you through applying for all eligible options.
Can I work part-time while studying abroad?
Yes. Most popular destinations including the UK, USA, Canada, and Australia allow international students on a valid student visa to work up to 20 hours per week during academic semesters, and full-time during scheduled term breaks.
What are the financial requirements to secure a student visa?
To secure a student visa, you must generally provide liquid bank statements or sanctioned education loan letters proving you possess sufficient funds to cover your first year's tuition fees plus a stipulated amount for living expenses set by the host country's government.
What happens during a typical embassy visa interview?
The visa officer primarily aims to verify your intent as a genuine student. They will ask questions regarding why you chose a specific university, how you intend to fund your education, and your post-graduation career plans to ascertain your ties to your home country.
Can my spouse or dependents travel with me?
This depends entirely on the destination country's current immigration policies and the level of your degree. For example, some countries only allow dependents if you are pursuing a postgraduate research degree or a PhD. We will provide specific advice based on your target country.
Does GlobalEdu help with securing education loans?
Yes, our dedicated financial team has exclusive partnerships with leading global and national financial institutions. We can assist you in comparing interest rates, applying for non-collateral loans, and accelerating the sanction process.
Category 3

Pre-Departure & Living Abroad

When should I start booking my student accommodation?
You should start looking for accommodation as soon as you receive your unconditional offer letter and apply for your visa. University dorms fill up extremely fast, and private student housing is usually booked out months before the intake begins.
Does GlobalEdu assist in finding off-campus housing?
Yes, we have tie-ups with verified global student accommodation providers. We can help you secure safe, affordable housing near your university campus without paying exorbitant broker fees.
What is covered in a pre-departure briefing?
Our pre-departure briefings cover everything from packing checklists and immigration protocols at the port of entry to opening an international bank account, getting a local SIM card, and adjusting to the local culture.
Should I carry cash or use a Forex card?
We recommend carrying a small amount of local currency in cash for immediate expenses like taxis or snacks upon arrival. For the bulk of your funds, a multi-currency Forex card is much safer and offers better exchange rates.
How do I arrange for health insurance abroad?
Health insurance is mandatory for international students. In countries like the UK, you pay an Immigration Health Surcharge during the visa process. In countries like the USA, we will help you purchase university-approved private health insurance plans.
Can you help book my international flight tickets?
Yes, through our travel partners, we can assist you in finding the most direct student-friendly flights, which often include extra baggage allowances specifically designed for students relocating for long-term studies.
Category 4

Careers & Post-Graduation

Can I stay and work in the country after I graduate?
Yes, most major destinations offer a post-study work visa. For example, the UK offers a 2-year Graduate Route, Canada offers up to a 3-year Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP), and Australia offers 2 to 4 years depending on your degree and location.
What is the OPT/STEM extension in the USA?
Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows international students in the US to work for 12 months after graduation. If your degree is classified as STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Math), you can apply for an additional 24-month extension, giving you 3 years total.
Will the university guarantee me a job after I graduate?
No university guarantees job placements. However, top universities have robust career service departments, host extensive career fairs with top employers, and provide massive alumni networks that heavily facilitate your job hunt.
Does studying abroad lead to Permanent Residency (PR)?
Studying abroad is an excellent pathway to PR, particularly in countries like Canada and Australia, where local education and work experience award you significant points in their respective immigration systems. It is not an automatic process, however.
Do employers in my home country value a foreign degree?
Yes, highly. Degrees from accredited international universities—especially in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia—are globally recognized. They indicate to employers that you have high cross-cultural competence, independence, and strong communication skills.
Do you provide alumni networking opportunities?
Yes. GlobalEdu hosts an exclusive alumni portal where recently graduated students can connect with our past successful students who are now working in top-tier companies across the globe. This provides invaluable mentorship and referral opportunities.
